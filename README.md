# Perfectionism and Workaholism: A Survey Study

## Overview
Is self-oriented perfectionism (perfectionism born from internal high standards) or socially prescribed perfectionism (perfectionism born from perceived external pressure) more strongly associated with workaholism?

## Data
Original survey of 32 participants, recruited by the team, combining the Multidimensional Perfectionism Scale (MPS, scoring participants for self-oriented and socially prescribed perfectionism) and the Work Addiction Risk Test (WART). Responses are not included to protect participant privacy.

## Methods
* Cleaned and scored the survey responses in Python (pandas)
* Explored the relationships between each perfectionism type and workaholism with correlations, scatterplots, and boxplots (seaborn, matplotlib)

## Key Findings
Both perfectionism types were positively correlated with workaholism, about equally (r = 0.52 for self-oriented, r = 0.51 for socially prescribed). With a small, self-reported sample and no significance testing, results are exploratory.
